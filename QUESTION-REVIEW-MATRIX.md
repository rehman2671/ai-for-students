# Question Review Matrix

Use this matrix after real learner sessions. A revision is approved only when session evidence shows repeated confusion or a genuine ambiguity.

| Question ID | Learning objective | Correct-answer rationale | What to observe | Revision status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| q1 | Define audience, length and practice format | A clear goal turns a broad topic into a learnable task | Do learners understand why the detailed request is stronger? | Baseline; needs learner evidence |
| q2 | Specify output structure and source boundary | Structure makes notes usable and tied to the learner’s material | Do learners confuse “better” with a measurable output? | Baseline; needs learner evidence |
| q3 | Give relevant context while retaining author control | Assignment goal, audience and criteria produce targeted feedback | Do learners understand why a full rewrite is not the best learning move? | Baseline; needs learner evidence |
| q4 | Use audience, time and presentation constraints | Constraints make the outline fit its real context | Do learners identify time and structure as useful constraints? | Baseline; needs learner evidence |
| q5 | Treat AI output as a lead, not evidence | Original-source checking is required before sharing facts | Do learners mistake confident wording for verification? | Baseline; needs learner evidence |
| q6 | Use AI as a tutor rather than an answer substitute | Hints and next-step support preserve the learner’s thinking | Do learners recognize the academic-integrity and learning value? | Baseline; needs learner evidence |

## Revision requirements

When revising a question, keep one unambiguous correct answer, preserve the original learning objective, keep explanations concrete, avoid culturally narrow assumptions and add a new row to the decision record. Re-run the revised question with at least three additional learners before treating it as stable.
