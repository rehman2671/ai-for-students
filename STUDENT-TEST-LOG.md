## Session instructions

Ask the learner to complete one game without coaching. Observe silently until they ask for help. After completion, ask three questions: “What did this game teach you?”, “Which case felt unclear?”, and “How did the difficulty feel?” Record the answers without identifying details.

## Revision rule

Do not change an answer merely because a learner selected it. Revise a question when the confusion is repeated, the wording permits two reasonable interpretations, or the learner cannot explain the intended takeaway. Preserve the learning objective and re-test the revised question with at least three additional learners.
