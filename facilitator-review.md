# AI for Students — Facilitator Review Worksheet

Use this worksheet with five to ten anonymous student sessions before treating the expanded field-game extensions as fully reviewed. Do not record names, emails, answer histories or other identifying information.

| Review area | What to inspect | Evidence to record | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field-game authorship | Each extension is specific to its game skill and is not a repeated template. | Scenario ID, confusing wording, proposed revision. | Pending facilitator review |
| Learning objective | The scenario teaches one observable AI habit. | Objective confirmed or rewritten. | Pending facilitator review |
| Difficulty | The intended age band and level match the cognitive load. | Starter/building/stretch judgement and reason. | Pending student sessions |
| Accessibility | Keyboard focus order, visible focus, labels, disabled states, reduced motion and readable contrast. | Device/browser, issue, severity, correction. | Code checks plus user QA pending |
| Replayability | Start, complete, play again, choose another game and guest entry all work. | Route, step, result and any stuck state. | Guest Source Hunt spot check complete; library sweep pending |
| Content accuracy | Explanations are correct, bounded and do not imply AI certainty. | Source or expert note for any correction. | Pending facilitator review |
| Privacy | No identity or answer data is included in anonymous feedback. | Confirmed fields only. | Implemented; recheck during sessions |
